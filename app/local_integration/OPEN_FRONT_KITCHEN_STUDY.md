# Independent open-front kitchen study

This path is not production generation, constructor127 provenance, or delivery authority.

## Proven standalone layers

- `open_front_kitchen_study.py` builds a new module schema from the bound 117 baseline. The exact allowed delta removes `front.right`, expands the permanent arch to the existing width, and adds its exact threshold floor. Appliances, activities, motion assumptions, narrow port and approach are retained.
- `open_front_kitchen_check.py` independently reconstructs that allowed delta, checks the original baseline, actual arch/host partition, ownership/role floor, retained route sweeps and fresh operations on the new module. Passing means only `PASS_MODULE_STUDY_ONLY`.
- `open_front_kitchen_place.py` places the new module in A/B with a rigid translation/reflection. Exact coordinate tests cover solids, hosts, apertures, fixtures, activities, ports, aprons and operation points/vectors. Passing does not establish safety against neighboring rooms.

The expanded threshold is not a full-width 0.7m approach. Retained route validity is not route optimality. Source113 ergonomics remain hypothetical.

## New-origin assembly under verification

`open_front_world_assembly.py` is an isolated assembler with a new model schema. `open_front_world_origin.py` binds exact raw input, proposals, core, model and source snapshots and deterministically replays that assembler. It does not emit or reuse CONSTRUCTION_TRACE127. Domain is 5..25 per axis, two units, explicit double+twin two-bedroom programme, FRONT_REAR only.

## Unresolved whole-gate dependencies

The historical kitchen checker binds schema117 and the old producer. Its placement checker dispatches schemas; named checks and Q11 import that placement checker and recognized replay paths again. Consequently, a new module/origin alone is not a whole-physical pass. A future independent whole checker must preserve every applicable geometry, operation, source, Q11, privacy, route and named-rule obligation with explicit new-schema adapters. Never filter historical errors or relabel historical provenance to obtain a pass.

The study workflow retains the historical checker result unfiltered as a diagnostic, not as new-contract admission. All accepted/display/delivery/qualification states remain false or UNKNOWN. The four current functional UNKNOWNs remain open.
